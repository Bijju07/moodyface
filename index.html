The face model reads 7 basic expressions: happy, sad, angry, surprised, fearful, disgusted, neutral. Moody, Love and Sexy are fun blends made from those scores, so treat them as a game, not a real reading of your feelings. Eyes, eyebrows, lips and nose are traced from 68 face points. The model has no ear points, so ears are dashed estimates beside the face. The photo stays on your device.
